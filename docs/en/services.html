Our services

More than safe deposit rental.

Safe deposit rental

Over 3,000 boxes in five sizes across two underground vaults. Dedicated zones for larger items. Contents stay confidential.

24/7 access

Standard hours are 10:00–19:00, but after-hours access can be arranged with management. The service is charged by tariff.

The Deal Box — escrow safe

A secure way to guarantee a deal between parties. A three-party agreement is signed and Rīgas Seifi holds the keys as deal guarantor until all conditions are met.

Guarded transport

Escorted transfer of valuables to or from the vault. Pricing depends on security level, vehicle and distance.

Confidential meeting room

An equipped room for signing deals or confidential talks before placing valuables. No video surveillance is recorded in this room.
