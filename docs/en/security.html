Security without compromise

Bank-grade security, without the bank.

The vault

  • Two underground reinforced-concrete vaults — over 100 m²
  • Isolated rooms: breaking in through the walls is ruled out
  • Rosengrens equipment (Sweden, founded in 1886)
  • Noise and vibration detectors inside the walls
  • Video surveillance and a fire-safety system
  • Round-the-clock armed security by SP Leģions with a rapid-response team

Three levels protect access to every box

The guard's mechanical key

The first key stays with the vault's security staff and is needed every time a box is opened.

Your mechanical key

The second key is issued to you alone. The box opens with both keys together.

Electronic PIN

An AES-encrypted PIN with protection against code interception. 100,000,000 combinations. After 3 wrong attempts — a 5-minute lockout, then longer.

Certificates: EN 1300 class C · BSI · ECB·S

Why 1,000+ clients have chosen us in 9 years

Banks in Latvia and around the world are winding down safe deposit services. We have been working since 2017 and became the first private vault in the Baltics precisely because we see the need: people and businesses want a reliable place for their valuables — without bank restrictions, queues or opening hours.
